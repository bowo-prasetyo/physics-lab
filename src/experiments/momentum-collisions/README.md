# Momentum and collisions

## Learning sequence
1. Commit to predictions on the shared prediction cards: equal carts swapping velocities and sticking together (Beginner), which quantities are conserved when 0 < e < 1 (Intermediate), and a heavy cart hitting a light one (Advanced). Each explanation unlocks only after the collision that tests it (`predictions.ts`). Results stay hidden until the carts have collided.
2. Vary masses, signed initial velocities and coefficient of restitution.
3. Compare velocity, total momentum, impulse and translational kinetic energy.
4. Identify elastic, inelastic and perfectly inelastic limits.
5. Explain where kinetic energy goes when it is not retained as translational kinetic energy.

## Mathematical depth
- Beginner: momentum and conservation in an isolated system.
- Intermediate: momentum conservation plus the coefficient of restitution.
- Advanced: center-of-mass motion, equal-and-opposite impulses, reduced mass and kinetic-energy loss.

## Model contract
One-dimensional, isolated, constant-mass, two-body impact. External impulse during impact is neglected. The coefficient of restitution lies in [0, 1]. Rotation, multidimensional contact, deformation details, sound, heat and fragmentation are not resolved. Energy not retained as translational kinetic energy is transferred to internal energy, sound or deformation; it is not destroyed.
